# Naruto signature lines

Sasuke, Sakura, Kakashi and Hinata now have their own short canonical excerpts rather than borrowing Naruto's signature line. The original Naruto entry is unchanged. All four sources are the official Naruto site's birthday columns, which reproduce the relevant manga panels and wording.

| Character | Excerpt | Official source / manga location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sasuke Uchiha | ...But what I do have is determination. | [Official Sasuke column](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1856), volume 1, chapter 4. |
| Sakura Haruno | Now it's your turn...to watch my back!! | [Official Sakura column](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1743), volume 6, chapter 53. |
| Kakashi Hatake | What is even more important now is teamwork! | [Official Kakashi column](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1903), volume 2, chapter 8. |
| Hinata Hyuga | I'm here because I want to be. | [Official Hinata column](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1668), volume 47, chapter 437. |

Each excerpt is at most ten words. Manga capitals are normalized to sentence case; Sakura's line-break ellipses are collapsed for display. These remain exact-word excerpts, with the original character attribution, and are distinct from the original Dawnstudy study reminders. The `quoteNote` field preserves manga context in the shared catalog. The other 76 records, including Naruto's existing line, are unchanged.
