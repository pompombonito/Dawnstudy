# Kaoru Hana companions: references and art direction

Five characters from *The Fragrant Flower Blooms with Dignity* (Kaoru Hana wa Rin to Saku) join the shared profile-icon and companion catalog. All five use original, locally rendered colored SVG interpretations with transparent surroundings. The existing 75 character records are preserved.

## Primary references inspected

- [Official anime character sheets](https://kaoruhana-anime.com/character/): the five expression sheets were inspected visually for hair silhouette, eye color, eyelids, lashes, resting expression, and school/casual clothing.
- [Official Blu-ray/DVD mini-character merchandise](https://kaoruhana-anime.com/bddvd/special.html): the Animate mini-character acrylic stands and the Toranoana set were inspected visually. These are explicitly labeled official mini-character goods on the anime site.
- [Complete six-character official chibi keychain sheet](https://kaoruhana-anime.com/assets/img/bddvd/special/toranoana.jpg): used to compare head/body ratio, character silhouettes, restrained versus lively expressions, and the normal school outfits together.
- [Princess Cafe licensed mini-character can badges](https://www.pricafe.shop/view/item/000000016005?category_page_id=ct1545): inspected for a second chibi rendition of the six characters. Their seasonal Halloween costumes are not used for the Dawnstudy school outfits.

Reference images were downloaded to temporary storage for visual review. No merchandise image, watermark, product backing, badge circle, or remote image URL is rendered in the website. The catalog's legacy `bg` color remains metadata; it is not a painted background.

## Dialogue

Kaoruko's short canonical line is: “I've never once thought you were scary, Rintaro-kun.” (8 words). It is quoted verbatim in [Aniplex Inc.'s official Netflix launch press release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-fragrant-flower-blooms-with-dignity-blossoms-on-netflix-us-302548199.html), under the story synopsis. Only Kaoruko owns this quote in the catalog. The companion helper can share it with the other four characters while preserving Kaoruko's actual speaker attribution.

Each of the five also has a distinct original study reminder, stored as `studyLine`. Those reminders are written for Dawnstudy and are not presented as canonical dialogue. No unverified lines from generated quote collections or fan recollections were added.
