# Chibi expression references: first five series

Research date: 2026-11-01. These are visual drawing notes for original SVG interpretations, not redistributed product images. The linked licensed images were downloaded only to a temporary research folder and inspected visually. A chibi reference is not evidence that a character always makes one expression; the recommendations below deliberately select a recognizable expression from that character's range.

## Black Clover

Visually inspected the official site's [2023 AMNIBUS chibi acrylic lineup](https://bclover.jp/season1/goods/item.php?id=1017628), [2017 chibi bonus illustrations](https://bclover.jp/season1/news/detail.php?id=1056415), and [2026 AMNIBUS chibi illustration cards](https://bclover.jp/season1/goods/item.php?id=1023833). The last collection includes Charmy. The cards show more face detail than the small stand lineup.

| Character | Eyes and brows | Mouth and face recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asta | Broad green eyes with hard, inward-slanting brows; irises smaller than the whole eye. | Determined toothy grin or open shout; compact cheek shape. Avoid a delicate closed smile. |
| Yuno | Narrow gold eyes; nearly horizontal upper lids and low straight brows. | Tiny flat mouth, cool neutral expression; a narrower lower face than Asta. |
| Noelle Silva | Large purple almond eyes with heavy outer lashes; slight upward tilt. | Small pout/firm mouth and raised proud chin. Use restrained blush rather than a universal rosy-cheek smile. |
| Yami Sukehiro | Tired, drooping upper lids and small irises; slightly uneven/relaxed brows. | Loose neutral mouth or crooked smirk; broader adult jaw, stubble. The stand image shows a distinctly sleepy expression. |
| Charmy Pappitson | Very round mint-green eyes, long upper lashes, high arched brows. | Big open delighted food smile, round plump cheeks. Her smiling expression is appropriate; its scale and shape should differ from Asta. |

| Character | Eyes and brows | Mouth and face recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eren Yeager | Round teal eyes with strong inward angled brows and a direct stare. | Small firm/tense mouth, not a friendly arc; slightly squared cheeks. |
| Mikasa Ackerman | Large dark-gray almond eyes; flatter upper lids and sparse lower lashes. | Tiny neutral or slightly downturned mouth, calm serious face. |

| Character | Eyes and brows | Mouth and face recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ken Kaneki | Heterochromia: viewer-left normal gray eye, viewer-right red iris in black ghoul sclera. Heavy, tired eyelids. | Quiet serious face; the mask supplies its own teeth. An eyepatch must cover the viewer-left normal eye, leaving the red eye visible. |

## Practical drawing constraints

- Preserve masks and hair occlusion; do not draw matching full eyes on top of them.
- Use independent upper/lower eyelid paths, iris size, pupil shape, highlight count, brow angle and mouth shape. Changing iris color alone is insufficient.
- Reduce or remove the universal pink blush for stern/tired characters.
- Keep cheerful smiles where references support them (especially Charmy, Hange, Orihime, Hide), with distinct open mouths, cheeks, or eye shapes.
- Reference photos remain outside the repository; no product image is shipped as Dawnstudy artwork.
