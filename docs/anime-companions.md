# Anime profile icons and companions

80 original SVG chibi interpretations: five characters from each of 16 series, including *The Fragrant Flower Blooms with Dignity*. See the [new cast and official chibi references](kaoruhana-companions.md). The artwork is drawn in code and bundled locally; no remote image requests or copied screenshots are used.

## Short signature lines

These brief excerpts are displayed in quotation marks with character attribution. English dub, subtitle, and manga wording can differ. Levi’s line is an English translation of the Japanese line in the linked licensed campaign. The Sakamoto line is attributed to the family rule. The Gojo line uses the revised English manga wording. Gon’s “Jajanken!” and Bleach’s “Bankai!” are signature technique calls.

Original study encouragement is separate from these quotations and is never presented as dialogue from the series. Characters without a verified suitable personal line share a credited signature line from their own series, keeping the actual speaker’s name. All 80 companions therefore have both original encouragement and sourced series dialogue. Lines appear every 150 seconds while the page is visible and the dock is closed; quiet mode pauses them. Quoted companions alternate an encouragement with a signature line.

## Implementation

The single catalog lives in `backend/anime-characters.json` so it is included by Render’s backend root directory. Frontend pickers and backend profile validation consume the same IDs. Both avatar and companion IDs persist with the profile. Original study lines and short excerpts run locally and do not make AI calls. Companion AI chat and its existing help behavior are unchanged.

## Expression and transparency revision

All 80 portraits now have individual eye contours, iris/pupil proportions, brows, mouths, jaw shapes, and selective blush. Stern, sleepy, anxious and masked characters retain those distinctions. SVG background tiles, circles, sparkles and borders have been removed; the icons have transparent surroundings.

Visual audits: [Black Clover through Tokyo Revengers](chibi-reference-a.md), [Slime through Naruto](chibi-reference-b.md), [JJK through Yona](chibi-reference-c.md). These link actual licensed chibi references and explicitly identify the few character-sheet fallbacks.

The follow-up art direction also uses the user's three supplied style references: a chibi expression chart, a colored deadpan hoodie chibi, and broad-cheek line art. The revised SVGs keep character colors, use flatter fills and clearer dark outlines, and omit decorative backgrounds. The references guide proportions and expression readability rather than replacing individual character traits.


## Character motion

CSS animates visible eyes, subtle gaze, existing mouth expressions, hair, and headwear without per-frame JavaScript. Each character has a stable phase to avoid synchronized blinking. Hidden eyes and covered mouths stay hidden. Separate nested SVG groups preserve placement and proportions while hair and hats move. A shared visibility observer pauses offscreen avatars and hidden tabs; reduced-motion preferences disable animation. The same eye/mouth motion is available on the existing animal, character, and portrait icons, with hat or hair motion where present.
