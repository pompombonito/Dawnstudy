# Chibi visual reference audit — Slime, MHA, Sakamoto, One Piece, Naruto

Researched 2026-11-01. The observations below come from visually inspecting actual manufacturer/licensed chibi images, not just the regular anime character sheets. Reference images were downloaded only into a temporary QA folder and inspected locally; they are not product assets. The SVG portraits should reinterpret the observed facial distinctions.

All five manufacturers' main product photographs were inspected together at readable size. They show clear differences in eye aperture, pupil/iris proportion and mouth shape despite sharing chibi proportions.

## Sakamoto Days

All five are present in the visually inspected [MegaHouse Buddy-Colle licensed rubber mascot sheet](https://en.megahobby.jp/products/rubber-mascot-buddy-collection-sakamoto-days).

| Character | Observed facial distinction / suitable direction |
| --- | --- |
| Taro Sakamoto | Opaque white round lenses obscure the eyes; small rectangular moustache/teeth detail, tiny mouth, broad round cheeks. Avoid visible giant sparkling eyes behind transparent glasses. |
| Shin Asakura | Small blue irises, strong straight brows pulled inward, compressed lower lids, annoyed open mouth or short frown. His scowl is distinct from Lu's comic grin. |
| Lu Shaotang | One eye tightly winked; the other large round red iris; a squared clench-tooth mouth. A laughing closed-eye expression is another shown option. |
| Yoichi Nagumo | Relaxed curved closed eyes and a loose open laugh; a softer cheek shape. A carefree expression suits him, rather than Shin's forceful stare. |
| Osaragi | Very heavy nearly horizontal upper lids, small dark eyes and a tiny circular O mouth. Expression reads blank/distracted, not cheerful. |

## Naruto

All five actual Nendoroids and their face-plate closeups are on the [Good Smile Naruto special page](https://special.goodsmile.info/naruto/). Standard and alternate plates were inspected; the suggested portrait can choose a recognizable alternate expression instead of forcing the same smile.

| Character | Observed facial distinction / suitable direction |
| --- | --- |
| Naruto | Round blue irises, strongly inward brows, wide squared tooth grin and clear whisker lines. Determined and boisterous rather than a gentle closed smile. |
| Sasuke | Low straight upper lids, dark gray irises, restrained lower lids. Alternate plate has a level/frowning mouth. Give him a cool unamused stare. |
| Sakura | Green eyes with thin tapered outer lashes and arched brows. Her comic anger plate uses blank pale eyes and a huge angular shouting mouth. |
| Kakashi | Visible eye is a very narrow half-lid with tiny gray pupil; other eye hidden by protector. Alternate happy plate closes the visible eye to a crescent. |
| Hinata | Nearly pupil-less pale lavender eyes with distinct soft circular iris rings and upper lashes; shy worried mouth and cheek blush on embarrassed plate. |

No product images were copied into the website. These observations are reference directions for original vector redraws.
